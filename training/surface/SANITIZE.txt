Text files under training/surface are the hub's files at the commit in SOURCE, byte for byte, except the files listed
below, in which internal names were replaced as bb72/SANITIZE.txt describes, each as an exact replacement. No number,
rule, argument or result changed.
- Paths. The cluster scratch directory became <cluster-scratch> and the home work directory ~/work (the defaults of
  ICMP_CKPT, ICMP_LABELS, ICMP_NV and ICMP_SHOTS).
- Hosts. The Windows workstation's nickname became "the Windows workstation".
- Names. The coordinating role became "the study lead"; a run name before a design, an engine or a venv became
  the thing itself ("the RRStream design", "the S8 engine", "the studies' venv_gpu", "the cu128 build").
- Cluster jobs. Alpine job ids became "an Alpine job" or were dropped ("its Alpine copy").
The comments keep their references to the research repository's notes (arch/README.md and its sections,
aux_a/README.md, SETTINGS, ASSUMPTIONS). Those notes are not part of this release; the facts the training needs are in
../LINEAGE.md and ../TRAINING.md.
