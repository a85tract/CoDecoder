Text files under training/bb are the hub's files at the commits in SOURCE, byte for byte, except the files listed
below, in which internal names were replaced as bb72/SANITIZE.txt and cascade/SANITIZE.txt describe, each as an exact
replacement. No number, rule, argument or result changed. bb/code.py, bb/cascade_net.py, bb/cascade/noise.py and
qnd/circuits.py are cascade/'s sanitized copies of the same files (the same commit).
- Paths. The cluster account became <user> and its scratch directory <cluster-scratch>; the home work directory of
  fetch_relay.sh's default became ~/work, as the QND_RELAY default of qnd/circuits.py.
- Hosts. The Windows workstation that trained C6' is "the Windows workstation" (its launcher's path is dropped).
- Names. The coordinating role became "the study lead"; the run names of the Cascade comparison became their role (the
  noise-model fit, the training stage) and the run labels in the job files' headings were dropped. Names that are
  identifiers stay (the --w3-stream and --w3-monitor options, the w3*_ job files, the W4 variable and the cascade_w*
  directories).
- Notes. References to the Cascade re-implementation's internal notes, which are not part of this release, were
  dropped or reworded. References to bb/DESIGN.md, bb/PLAN.md, bb/STAGE3_PLAN.md and the gross plans stay (bb72/,
  gross/ and gross_stage4/ hold them), as do two to research notes that are not released (SETTINGS.md,
  interp/REPORT.md).
- Cluster jobs. Alpine job ids became "an Alpine job" (a dependency on one: "on that segment's job").
